## Beauty and lifestyle spins on the same shapes
- **1-star reviews of my favorite beauty products** (the same humor + debate shape)
- **What my best friend's makeup bag has vs mine** (the same contrast joke)
- **Influence or deinfluence my beauty cart** (the same comment-bait)
- **Read with me + skincare:** a face mask on, a candle, real sound, no talking

## Skip: these match formats that sank on all 5 accounts
Personal roundups and logs: 10 favorite reads, how much I read in a week, reading goals check-in, my week in books, books on my TBR, what's new on my e-reader, what I borrowed at the library, how I rate my books, my signed books, collage of the book I just read, readathon vlog.

Also skip **bookstore vlog** and **go to the library with me** as plain "come with me" vlogs. Yours landed between 0.6x and 1.3x. They can work if you give them a hook sentence: "I have $20 and 30 minutes in a Chicago bookstore" beats "bookstore vlog".
