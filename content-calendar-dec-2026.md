# Instagram Content Calendar: December 2026 (v3, books × beauty × lifestyle)

**Account:** @lifewithbrianaa · romance books × beauty × lifestyle · Chicago · 68 followers, median 205 views per Reel
**Cadence:** 2 posts a week, Tuesday + Friday, plus one **breaking-moment slot** you can use any day
**Built from:** `data/swipe.md`: 76 Reels across 5 accounts

## The mix

| | Books-led | Beauty-led | Lifestyle-led |
|---|---|---|---|
| Posts | Dec 1, Dec 11 | Dec 4, Dec 8, Dec 22 | Dec 15, Dec 18, Dec 25 |

Most posts blend two or three pillars. The research shows **beauty and lifestyle do best with a specific angle**: a constraint (Chicago winter, under $20), a joke, or a tie-in to books. A plain product comparison landed at 0.76x for Jade, and your plain "skincare routine" and "day in my life" Reels landed at 0.2x and 0.5x. Your iPhone unboxing (7.7x) shows lifestyle can travel when there's a specific, searchable thing in it.

**Rules for every post:**
- **On-screen hook = a full sentence**, not a label. ("skincare routine" got 48 views; a full-sentence version is what this calendar uses.)
- **Faceless or hands-first opening frame** on product and humor Reels (test it). Your 3 best Reels opened that way.
- **No roundups, no PR unboxings, no single-book reviews.**
- **Only real products, real prices, real places.** Every product, price and Chicago spot below is a slot for something you actually use or go to. Change any number that isn't true.
- **One ask per post.**

The hook scores come from `hookscore.py`. It's good at catching weak hooks but can't predict hits, and it under-scores humor.

## Week 1

**Hook, scored 66:** "I said one more chapter at 11pm. It is 2am and he still hasn't confessed."
- **Shot:** Hands only. An open book under a lamp, then a phone screen showing 2:00. No talking. 6–8 seconds, so it loops.

- **Ask:** Comment the book

### Fri Dec 4 · Carousel · BEAUTY · narrow, saveable list
**Cover, scored 78 STRONG:** "5 products keeping my skin alive through a Chicago winter, all under $20."
- **Slides 2–6:** One product per slide, held up against a wintry window or with snow outside if you can. Big text: the product name, the price, and one line on what it fixes ("for the dry patches the wind gives me").
- **Slide 7:** "Save this before the next cold snap"
- **Before posting:** Every product has to be one you really use, at its real price. If one costs more than $20, change the cover line.
- **Caption:** `Chicago wind does not care about my skin barrier 🥶 What's your winter must-have?`
- **Ask:** Save
- **Why this shape:** "Standalone romantasy" did 41x for Skye because of the narrow constraint. "Chicago winter" plus "under $20" does the same job for beauty, and people search for it.

## Week 2

### Tue Dec 8 · Reel · BEAUTY × BOOKS · makeup look from a book cover
**Hook, scored 52:** "A full face of makeup using only the 3 colors on this romance cover."
- **Shot:** Frame 1 is the book cover filling the screen. Pull the 3 colors out as swatches (on your hand or a palette), then quick cuts of each step, and end holding the book next to your face. Burn in captions; no talking needed.
- **Pick a cover with bold, makeup-friendly colors.** You need 3 distinct shades.
- **Caption:** `[Book title] but make it a makeup look 💄 Drop a cover for me to do next 👇`
- **Ask:** Suggest a cover. The suggestions become future posts, so this can run as a series.

### Fri Dec 11 · Carousel · BOOKS · narrow list + deadline
**Cover, scored 84 STRONG:** "5 romances under 300 pages you can finish before New Year's."
- **Slides 2–6:** One book per slide, photographed on your vanity or bed. Big text: the title, the page count, and one line in the form "read this if you want ___". Pick books you've actually read, and check each page count. Editions differ, so use the one on your copy.
- **Slide 7:** "Save this and still hit your reading goal 🎉"
- **Why this one:** It's the highest-scoring hook so far, and the deadline only works this month. It's the same narrow-list shape that did 41x for Skye, plus a reason to read now.
- **Caption:** `Still have time to hit your reading goal, and none of these are over 300 pages 📚⏳ Which one are you grabbing first?`
- **Ask:** Save

## Breaking-moment slot (any day it happens)

When something everyone is talking about happens, post within hours:

> **"Romance readers / beauty girls during [the moment]"** + one funny line on screen + you, or your hands, with a book or a product.

- Film it in under 10 minutes. Speed beats polish. This is the format behind Baddie's 74K-view Reel on a 1.3K account.
- Ideas to keep ready: a Chicago snow day, an app outage, an adaptation trailer, a viral beauty launch selling out.

## Stories that carry the mix
- **Nook progress** between Parts 1 and 3, with one poll per decision (lamp, mirror, chair).
- **"What's on my vanity today"**: one product, one honest sentence, a 1–5 emoji slider.
- **Question box:** "What's your must-have for Chicago winter skin?" Your followers' answers feed a January Part 2.
- **Current read** with a "rate it" slider.
