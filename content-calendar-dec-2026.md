**Rules that hold for every post** (from the swipe file):
- **On-screen hook = a full sentence**, not a label. Your top Reels averaged 11 words of on-screen text; your bottom ones averaged 3.5.
- **Faceless or hands-first opening frame** on the humor Reels (test it). Your 3 best Reels all opened that way.
- **No roundups, no PR unboxings, no single-book reviews.** They sank on all 5 accounts.
- **One ask per post.**

The hook scores come from `hookscore.py`. It's good at spotting weak hooks but can't predict hits, and it under-scores humor. Baddie's 52x Reel only scored 42.

## Week 1: lead with what already works

### Tue Dec 1 · Reel · Relatable reader humor
**Hook (spoken and on screen), scored 66:** "I said one more chapter at 11pm. It is 2am and he still hasn't confessed."
- **Shot:** Hands only. An open book under a lamp, then a phone screen showing 2:00. No talking. Use a quiet, trending sound.
- **Length:** 6–8 seconds, so it loops.
- **Caption:** `Slow burn is a lifestyle and I am tired 😩 What book did this to you? 👇`
- **Ask:** Comment the book

### Fri Dec 4 · Carousel · Narrow, saveable list
**Cover (slide 1), scored 79 STRONG:** "4 standalone romances you can finish in one weekend. No series, no cliffhangers."
- **Slides 2–5:** One book per slide, photographed on your bed or vanity, with one line each in the form "read this if you want ___". Pick from books you've actually read. If you need ideas, Emily Henry's *Beach Read*, *Book Lovers* and *Happy Place* are all standalones.
- **Slide 6:** "Save this for your next free weekend"
- **Caption:** `For when you want the happily ever after by Sunday night 📚 Which one are you starting with?`
- **Ask:** Save

## Week 2: beauty, attached to books

### Tue Dec 8 · Reel · Annotation kit (beauty-style flat lay)
**Hook, scored 57:** "Everything I use to annotate spicy romance books, all under $30."
- **Shot:** Hands only, top-down. Lay the items out one at a time (tabs, highlighters, pens, sticky notes), then show your color key on a page. Shoot it like a makeup flat lay.
- **Before posting:** Check the $30 figure against your actual supplies and change it if needed. Don't post a number that isn't true.
- **Caption:** `Pink = swoon, red = spice, yellow = I screamed 🖍️ What's your color code?`
- **Ask:** Comment your color code (an easy comment to leave)

### Fri Dec 11 · Carousel · Debate
**Cover, scored 60:** "Are these 5 BookTok romances actually worth the hype?"
- **Slides 2–6:** The cover of a book you've read on each slide, a verdict in big text (**WORTH IT** / **SKIP** / **ONLY IF…**), and one honest sentence. Include at least one unpopular opinion. That's what gets people commenting.
- **Caption:** `Be honest, did I get one wrong? 👀`
- **Ask:** Comment to agree or argue

## Week 3: start the series

### Tue Dec 15 · Reel · Nook makeover, Part 1
**Hook, scored 75 STRONG:** "Part 1: turning this empty corner into my reading and vanity nook before Christmas."
- **Shot:** The empty corner as it is now, then quick cuts of your plan (a mood board on your phone, a sketch, the products in your cart). End on "follow for Part 2".
- **Why a series:** Baddie's library build was 5 of her top 6 Reels. People follow to see how it ends.
- **Caption:** `Day 1 of building my favorite spot in the apartment 🕯️📚 Bookshelf or vanity first?`
- **Ask:** Follow for Part 2

### Fri Dec 18 · Reel or carousel · Nook makeover, Part 2
**Hook:** "Part 2: the shelf is up. Now I need your help."
- **Shot:** The progress so far, plus 2 options for one decision (a lamp, a chair, a mirror). Ask people to vote.
- **Caption:** `Option 1 or option 2? I'm buying whichever wins 👇`
- **Ask:** Vote in the comments

## Breaking-moment slot (any day it happens)

When something everyone is talking about happens (an app outage, a celebrity engagement, an adaptation trailer, a snow day), post within hours:

> **"Romance readers during [the moment]"** + one funny line on screen + you, or your hands, with a book.

- Film it in under 10 minutes. Speed beats polish.
- This is the format behind Baddie's 74K-view Reel on a 1.3K account.
- Ideas to keep ready: "Romance readers when the WiFi goes out", "Romance readers on a snow day", "Romance readers when [adaptation] drops a trailer".

## Stories between posts
- A poll on the day you post ("Did you comment your fictional man yet?")
- Behind-the-scenes nook progress, so the series stays in view between parts
- Your current read with a "rate it 1–5" emoji slider
