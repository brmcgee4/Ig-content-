# Instagram Content Calendar — December 2026

**Niche:** Romance books × lifestyle × beauty
**Cadence:** 2 posts a week, on **Tuesday** (a Reel, for reach) and **Friday** (a carousel or photo, for saves and comments)
**Mix:** 3 books-led · 2 beauty-led · 3 a blend of both, so the account reads as one aesthetic, not three topics

Each week gets one Reel and one save-worthy post. Every caption ends with a question so people comment.

---

## Content pillars

| Pillar | What it is | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Read the Look** | Beauty looks, nails and outfits inspired by books and characters | Brings beauty and book audiences into one post |
| **Trope Talk** | Recommendations organized by trope (enemies-to-lovers, grumpy/sunshine…) | People save these and share them with friends |
| **Cozy Rituals** | Reading nook, skincare and a book, slow mornings | The lifestyle side, and a natural place for product mentions |
| **Shelf Life** | Wrap-ups, TBR piles, ratings | Gives followers a reason to come back regularly |

## Week 1

### Tue Dec 1 — Reel · Trope Talk
**Hook (on screen, first 2 sec):** "December reads based on your favorite trope 🎄"
**Shots:** Hold up 4–5 books one at a time while trope text pops on screen (enemies-to-lovers, fake dating, small-town Christmas, forced proximity, grumpy/sunshine). Use a trending soft/cozy audio clip.
**Caption:**
> Your December TBR, sorted by trope ❄️ Which one are you picking up first? Tell me your #1 trope and I'll recommend your next read 👇
**CTA:** Comment your trope

### Fri Dec 4 — Carousel · Cozy Rituals
**Hook (slide 1):** "My winter night-in routine (book included, obviously)"
**Slides:** 1) Cover photo: candle, book, face mask · 2) Skincare step 1 · 3) Skincare step 2 · 4) Tea or hot chocolate · 5) Current read, close-up · 6) Reading nook wide shot · 7) "Save this for your next night in"
**Caption:**
> The only plans I want this month 🕯️📖 Skincare, something warm, and a book I can't put down. What's your non-negotiable night-in item?
**CTA:** Save + comment

## Week 2

### Tue Dec 8 — Reel · Read the Look
**Hook:** "Doing my makeup as the main character of my current read"
**Shots:** Show the book cover, then a fast-cut makeup transition into the finished look, then hold the book next to your face. Use the colors on the cover as the palette.
**Caption:**
> If [book title] were a makeup look 💄 I pulled the palette straight from the cover. Should I make this a series? Drop a book for me to do next 👇
**CTA:** Suggest a book (this gives you ideas for future posts)

### Fri Dec 11 — Carousel · Trope Talk
**Hook (slide 1):** "Books to read if you want to feel something this holiday season"
**Slides:** One book per slide, with the cover photographed in your space plus a one-line "read this if…" (e.g., "read this if you want to ugly cry under a blanket"). Use 6–8 slides.
**Caption:**
> Emotional damage, but make it festive 🎁💔 Which of these is already on your shelf?
**CTA:** Save for later

## Week 3

### Tue Dec 15 — Reel · Cozy Rituals × Beauty
**Hook:** "Get ready with me for a bookstore date (with myself)"
**Shots:** A get-ready-with-me (GRWM) Reel: quick makeup, outfit, a walk into a bookstore or café, browsing, then the book haul at the end. Use a voiceover or text overlays.
**Caption:**
> Solo bookstore dates are self-care and I will not be taking questions ☕📚 What's the last book you bought on impulse?
**CTA:** Comment

### Fri Dec 18 — Carousel · Gift guide
**Hook (slide 1):** "Gift guide for the romance reader in your life"
**Slides:** Annotation tabs, a book sleeve, a candle, a lip balm or hand cream, a reading light, a fan-favorite book, cozy socks, and a "screenshot this and send it to someone" slide at the end.
**Caption:**
> Sending this to the person who still hasn't asked what I want 🎀 What's the best bookish gift you've ever gotten?
**CTA:** Share + save (gift guides get shared a lot in mid-December)

## Week 4

## Planning notes

- **Batch-film:** Shoot both posts for a week in one session (for example, on Sunday). The two Reels in Weeks 2 and 3 need makeup, so you could film them on the same day.
- **Stories between posts:** Post 2–3 Stories on the other days, such as a poll ("which book next?"), a page you're reading, or a product you're testing. This keeps you visible without extra feed posts.
- **Recurring series:** If the Dec 8 "makeup as a book character" Reel does well, make it a series you post every other week in January.
- **January preview:** Plan a "2027 reading goals + new-year skincare reset" carousel for the first week of January.
