# Bookmas 2026: 25 Days, Dec 1–25

**One post a day from Dec 1 to Christmas, like an advent calendar.** Romance books × beauty × Chicago lifestyle.

The 8 posts already in `content-calendar-dec-2026.md` stay as the bigger posts (★ below). Full shot notes for those are in that file. The 17 new days are mostly quick formats so that posting daily is doable.

## Why a series, and the honest caveats
- **A series gives people a reason to follow.** Baddie's library-build series was 5 of her top 6 Reels, and a numbered countdown works the same way.
- **The research didn't test posting every day.** Some days will land below your 205-view median, and that's fine. Track every post, and in January `/ig-audit` will show which Bookmas days actually worked.
- **This is a lot of work.** 25 posts is 3x your current pace. The batching plan below is what keeps it manageable, so don't skip it.

## Branding (same every day)
- **A title card on every post:** `BOOKMAS · DAY 7/25` in the same font and color, top-left, inside the safe zone. Skye's matching title cards are why her grid is recognizable at a glance.
- **Pick 2 colors** (e.g. deep red and cream) and use them on every cover.
- **Announce it in Stories on Sun Nov 30:** "25 days of Bookmas starts tomorrow. Romance, beauty, and Chicago cozy, every day until Christmas 🎄📚" with a countdown sticker set to Dec 1.

## Effort levels
- **Q (Quick):** under 15 minutes. One shot, or a text-on-photo carousel.
- **M (Medium):** about 30–60 minutes.
- **B (Big):** a filming session.

## Notes for the new days

- **Day 2 (Hallmark tropes):** One trope per slide, with a mock movie title ("Small-town baker, grumpy city lawyer, 1 snowstorm") and the trope name stamped on top. Quick to make in Canva.
- **Day 3 (face mask + chapter):** Real sound only: a page turning, the candle, quiet. Mel's "no trending audio" read-with-me did 2.8x.
- **Day 5 (Santa list):** A text carousel: "a man who communicates", "1 bed at the inn", "a grumpy neighbor who shovels my car out". Make your own list; these are just starters.
- **Day 6 (Christmas market):** Shoot at a Chicago holiday market you actually go to. Add the location tag, since people search for it.
- **Day 7 (movie → romance):** Only use books you've read and can honestly match to the movie.
- **Day 9 (snow day):** **Post this on the first real snow day**, whatever the date, and swap it with another Quick day. It's the breaking-moment format.
- **Day 10 (stocking stuffers):** Mix beauty and books (lip balm, tabs, a book light, a mini candle). Use real items and real prices. Posting before mid-December gives people time to buy.
- **Day 12 (1-star reviews):** Read real 1-star reviews of books you love and react. Show the review text, not the reviewer's name.
- **Day 16 (lip combos):** Use products you own. Each combo gets a trope name, a swatch and a lip photo.
- **Day 19 (wrapping):** Hands only, real sound. Paper chosen by trope: plaid = small town, black and gold = dark romance.
- **Day 21:** Dec 21 is the winter solstice, the longest night of the year.
- **Day 23 (last-minute gifts):** Only list things you've confirmed are in stores near you.
